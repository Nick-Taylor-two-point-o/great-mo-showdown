The rules.

  • Donate to either page. You can back both sides if you like chaos.
  • Voting closes at 11:59 pm on 30 November, NZ time.

Why I’m doing this.

Every dollar raised supports Movember’s work in men’s mental health and suicide prevention, helping Kiwis who are doing it tough. Some struggle openly, while others fight their battles behind closed doors.

So whether your donation condemns my wife to another year of questionable facial hair or restores some dignity to my upper lip, more importantly, it’ll help support fellow New Zealanders through some of life’s toughest moments.

Thanks in advance for your generosity!
